Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Photo Cut mobile application and this Photo Cut website handle information. It applies to Photo Cut, including the Android application identified by package name com.frainzzel.photocut.

Short version:

Photo Cut is designed to work without a Photo Cut account or backend. Photos are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to servers operated by Photo Cut. The app does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Information Photo Cut accesses

Photo Cut can access a photo only after you choose it through the device image picker. The app reads the selected image so it can inspect its dimensions, apply orientation, crop or grayscale settings you request, and generate the print-ready PDF.

Photo Cut does not scan your photo library in the background and does not upload the selected photo to Photo Cut servers.

2. Local processing

Image processing and PDF generation happen on your device. Photo Cut does not operate a cloud service for storing or processing your photos.

The generated PDF is held by the app for review and can be passed to another app or system service only when you explicitly choose to share or print it.

3. Sharing and printing

If you use the Share or Print actions, Photo Cut hands the generated PDF to the operating system and the app, print service or destination you select. Those third parties may process the PDF according to their own privacy policies. Photo Cut does not control how a third-party app, printer service or destination handles a file after you choose to send it there.

4. Purchases

Photo Cut can offer a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled by the platform store, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store may process account, transaction and payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Photo Cut receives purchase status and product information needed to unlock or restore the purchase. Photo Cut does not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Local app state and Android backup

Photo Cut stores a small amount of app state locally on your device, including whether the free final-PDF use has been consumed and a local cache of lifetime entitlement where applicable.

On Android, the marker indicating that the free final-PDF use has been consumed is eligible for Android system backup and device transfer. This backup is controlled by Android and Google according to your device and Google account backup settings. The local lifetime entitlement cache is excluded from that Photo Cut backup rule; purchase ownership is restored through the platform store.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking

Photo Cut does not include third-party advertising, behavioural analytics, or tracking SDKs. Photo Cut does not sell personal data.

7. Data retention and deletion

Selected photos are temporary job input and are not retained by Photo Cut on a developer-operated server. The app processes them in local app memory for the active workflow.

Generated PDFs remain under your control after you share, save or print them through your device. You can delete those files using the destination app or storage location you selected.

Local Photo Cut app state remains on the device until it is cleared by the operating system, you clear the app's data, or you uninstall the app. Android backups may remain according to Google's backup retention rules and your account settings.

Because Photo Cut does not provide accounts or operate a user-data backend, the developer generally has no server-side photo or profile data to delete on your behalf. You can remove local Photo Cut data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and remove exported PDFs from the destination where you saved them.

8. Photo Cut website

The Photo Cut website does not use Photo Cut analytics, advertising trackers or marketing cookies. The site is hosted on Netlify. As the hosting provider, Netlify may process standard web request information such as IP address, browser or device information, and request logs as needed to deliver and protect the site under Netlify's own privacy practices.

9. Security

Photo Cut reduces privacy risk by keeping photo processing local and by not operating an account or image-storage backend. When data is handed to Google Play, the Apple App Store, Android backup, a sharing destination or a print service, the security of that processing is governed by the relevant third party.

10. Children's privacy

Photo Cut does not intentionally collect personal information from children or adults through a Photo Cut account or backend. If the app is used by a child, any platform-level store, backup or sharing services remain subject to the applicable platform and account settings.

11. Changes to this policy

If Photo Cut's data handling changes, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant app release. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the current version.

12. Developer and privacy contact
