# Market thesis (pre-build)

Last reviewed: 2026-09-03

## Hypothesis

A meaningful group of users occasionally needs to print an image at a precise
physical size but does not want to use Word, Canva or a general graphics editor.
They value a narrow workflow and are willing to make a small one-time purchase.

## Existing paid behaviour

Examples observed during initial research:

- Print to Size on iOS: a paid app centred on physical print dimensions and page
  layout.
- Print Photo - Print to Size on Android: a paid app addressing the same job.
- Other photo-size apps use recurring subscriptions, and some reviews explicitly
  object to subscriptions for occasional use.

Reference listings:

- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/print-to-size/id949490225
- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tapuniverse.printphoto

These listings demonstrate an existing category and payment behaviour; they do
not guarantee that Photo Cut will acquire users profitably.

## Product angle

- Exact physical size, explained in one screenshot.
- One image repeated automatically rather than a free-form canvas.
- Transparent one-time unlock.
- No ads, account or cloud.
- Strong Spanish and English store keywords.

## Validation strategy without interviews

Launch a deliberately small version and measure:

- completed first layouts;
- preview-to-export rate;
- free-export completion;
- paywall views and lifetime purchases;
- search impressions and store-page conversion;
- support/review themes.

Do not expand scope before observing completed real jobs and purchases.
